# Support

SoundNinja is a community project in early beta. The fastest way to get help is GitHub.

## Report a bug or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub:

[github.com/marcus-universe/SoundNinja/issues](https://github.com/marcus-universe/SoundNinja/issues)

Include your OS, SoundNinja version (**Help → Check for Updates** or **Settings → About**), and steps to reproduce.

## Email

Maintainer contact: [contact@marcus-universe.de](mailto:contact@marcus-universe.de)

## Updates

In the app, use **Help → Check for Updates**. New builds also appear on the [Download page](/download) and on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/marcus-universe/SoundNinja/releases).
