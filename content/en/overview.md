# Overview

SoundNinja is an open-source soundboard for live streaming, chatting with friends, and podcast recording. It is built with Tauri v2 and Nuxt 4 so it stays light on RAM and disk.

The goal is simple: keep a huge sound library easy to scan, then play the right clip in milliseconds. The UI also works well as a dashboard in VR.

SoundNinja is still in early development. Features work, but not every platform is fully tested.

## Who it is for

- Streamers who need instant sound cues
- Friends who want a shared board for voice chat
- Podcasters who record clips and drop them onto a board
- Anyone who wants a customizable, local-first soundboard

## What you can do

- Build a soundboard from your own audio files
- Organize sounds in colored tabs, groups, and search
- Design the look with the Theme Creator
- Record PC audio or a microphone in the Record Editor
- Assign hotkeys to individual sounds
- Trigger sounds from [Bitfocus Companion](/docs/remote-control)
- Import and export a full soundboard to share with others
- Use GIFs or images on sound buttons
